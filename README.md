### 🔹 Etherna Immunotherapies (2021 – Present)
> *mRNA design · immunogenicity prediction · pipeline architecture*

- Architected an end-to-end **mRNA design & optimisation platform** integrating UTR discovery, RNA secondary-structure prediction, codon optimisation, and variant annotation
- Built **immunogenicity prediction pipeline** combining neoantigen identification (pVACtools, NetMHCpan), HLA typing, and miRNA off-target analysis
- Deployed **containerised Snakemake workflows** on HPC (Slurm) for reproducibility across multi-cohort datasets
- Delivered **interactive dashboards** (Dash, Plotly, PowerBI) consolidating multi-omic reporting for R&D and pharma partners

### 🔹 Vrije Universiteit Brussel — LMCT (2019 – 2020)
> *neoantigen identification · personalised cancer vaccines*

- Led WES-based neoantigen identification pipeline for personalised tumour vaccine design
- Co-authored publications in *Vaccines* (2020) and *Oncogene* (2019)

### 🔹 Vrije Universiteit Brussel — LMMO (2012 – 2018)
> *WES/WGS · hereditary breast cancer · EGFR mutations · NSCLC*

- High-throughput WES/WGS of hereditary breast cancer cohorts; identified **RAD17** as a novel predisposition gene
- Developed a generic **PCR-based EGFR-TK mutation detection assay** for NSCLC clinical diagnostics
- Characterised germline/somatic mutations, CNV profiles, and mutational signatures
- **EACR Travel Fellowship** — visiting researcher at MD Anderson Cancer Center (2012–13)
