## `$ history | grep experience`

### 🔹 Scientist — Bioinformatics &nbsp;·&nbsp; Etherna Immunotherapies, Belgium &nbsp;·&nbsp; `2021 – Present`

Etherna Immunotherapies is a clinical-stage RNA therapeutics company developing personalised cancer vaccines.

- Architected an **end-to-end mRNA design and optimisation platform** integrating UTR discovery, RNA secondary-structure prediction (ViennaRNA, RNAstructure), codon optimisation, and variant annotation — directly informing clinical-stage vaccine programmes
- Developed an **immunogenicity prediction pipeline** combining neoantigen identification (pVACtools, NetMHCpan), HLA typing, and miRNA off-target analysis (TargetScan, miRanda), shortening candidate prioritisation cycles for internal R&D and external pharma partners
- Deployed **containerised (Docker) Snakemake workflows** on HPC (Slurm), ensuring reproducibility and scalability across multi-cohort datasets
- Delivered **interactive reporting dashboards** (Dash, Plotly, PowerBI) consolidating multi-omic outputs for scientific and clinical stakeholders

<br/>

### 🔹 Scientific Collaborator &nbsp;·&nbsp; Lab. of Molecular & Cellular Therapy, VUB &nbsp;·&nbsp; `2019 – 2020`

- Led development of a **WES-based cancer neoantigen identification pipeline** integrating variant calling, HLA typing, and epitope prediction for personalised tumour vaccine design
- Co-authored peer-reviewed publications on neo-antigen mRNA vaccines (*Vaccines* 2020) and CRAF oncogene sensitivity to combined RAF/MEK inhibition (*Oncogene* 2019)

<br/>

### 🔹 Scientific Researcher &nbsp;·&nbsp; Lab. of Medical & Molecular Oncology, VUB &nbsp;·&nbsp; `2012 – 2018`

- Performed **high-throughput WES/WGS analysis** of hereditary breast cancer cohorts; identified **RAD17** and additional novel predisposition gene candidates — results published in *BMC Cancer* (2019, 2024) and *Int J Cancer* (2020)
- Developed a **generic PCR-based EGFR-TK mutation detection assay** for NSCLC, improving clinical diagnostic workflow sensitivity (*Appl Immunohistochem Mol Morphol* 2015)
- Characterised germline/somatic mutation landscapes, CNV profiles, and mutational signatures across multiple tumour types
- Awarded **EACR Travel Fellowship** for a visiting research stay at MD Anderson Cancer Center (2012–13); received Prijs Kankeronderzoek (2016) and two OECI Awards (2012, 2015)

<br/>

### 🔹 Lecturer & Programme Coordinator &nbsp;·&nbsp; Multiple Institutions, Nepal &nbsp;·&nbsp; `2006 – 2009`

- Designed and taught courses in biotechnology, bioinformatics, and microbial genetics across Tribhuvan University, St Xavier College, and Lord Buddha Education Foundation
- Directed departmental strategy and curriculum development; supervised laboratory practicals

<br/>

### 🔹 Research Trainee &nbsp;·&nbsp; Everest Biotech, Nepal &nbsp;·&nbsp; `2006`

- Antibody production workflow including goat immunisation, serum processing, and protein purification via affinity chromatography
